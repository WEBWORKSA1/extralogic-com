FAQ

Is ExtraLogic free?

Yes. All daily puzzles, games and the reasoning test are free.

Is the reasoning test an IQ test?

No. It's a practice reasoning assessment for learning and fun, not a clinical IQ test.

Do I need an account?

No. Streaks and stats are stored in your browser. Pro accounts with syncing are coming.

Can I embed ExtraLogic puzzles on my site?

Embeds are coming for partners. Get in touch to join the pilot.

How do I report a wrong answer?

Use the contact form and choose "Report a puzzle issue". We fix confirmed errors within 48 hours.

Is this website or domain for sale?

Serious inquiries are welcome. Use the link at the top of every page or see Acquire this site.
