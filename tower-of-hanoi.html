Tower of Hanoi

Move the whole tower to the right peg, one disk at a time, never placing a larger disk on a smaller one.

Moves: 0 / min 150:00

How to play

Click a peg to pick up its top disk, then click another peg to drop it. The minimum number of moves for n disks is 2ⁿ − 1.

The recursive trick: to move n disks, move n−1 disks out of the way, move the largest disk, then move the n−1 disks back on top.

FAQ

Why is it used in computer science?

It's the textbook example of recursion and exponential growth: 64 disks would take 2⁶⁴ − 1 moves.

Is there a pattern for odd and even disk counts?

Yes. The smallest disk always cycles in the same direction, and which direction depends on whether n is odd or even.
