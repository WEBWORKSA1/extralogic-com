Privacy Policy

Last updated: September 2026

What we collect

Information you give us: when you submit a form (newsletter, test report, contest registration, business inquiry, donation pledge, application), we receive the fields you fill in. Automatically: standard analytics data such as pages viewed, device type and approximate location, if you consent. In your browser: game progress, streaks and preferences are stored locally on your device and are not sent to us.

How we use it

To reply to you, deliver what you asked for, run contests, send newsletters you opted into, improve the site, and show ads that keep ExtraLogic free. We never sell your personal data.

Cookies and advertising

Third-party vendors, including Google, use cookies to serve ads based on your previous visits to this and other websites. Google's use of advertising cookies enables it and its partners to serve ads based on your visits to this site and/or other sites on the Internet. You can opt out of personalised advertising at Google Ads Settings or aboutads.info. If you choose "Essential only" in our cookie banner, we request non-personalised ads only.

Service providers

Forms are delivered through a form-processing service; payments go through the provider you choose (e.g. PayPal, Stripe) under their own privacy policies; videos are embedded in YouTube's privacy-enhanced mode.

Your rights

You can ask us to access, correct or delete your data, or unsubscribe at any time, via the contact form. GDPR, UK GDPR, CCPA and PIPEDA rights are honoured.

Children

ExtraLogic is suitable for all ages, but we don't knowingly collect personal information from children under 13 without parental consent. Parents can contact us to remove such data.

Changes

We'll post updates on this page with a new date.
