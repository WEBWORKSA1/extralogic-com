Business FAQ

Can we use our own questions?

Yes. Assessments and events can mix our library with your own custom puzzles and branding.

Do you support remote teams across time zones?

Yes. Self-paced formats run over a window of 24 hours to 2 weeks, with a live leaderboard.

Is the assessment a validated psychometric instrument?

Our standard assessments are skills screens, not clinical tests. For high-stakes hiring, use them alongside structured interviews. We can advise on best practice.

How do we pay?

Card, bank transfer or invoice/PO for organizations.
