Submit your puzzle

Original riddles, logic grids, liar puzzles and sequences. Accepted puzzles are credited to you, and puzzles picked for contest sets are paid.

Guidelines

  • Original work only (or public domain, with the source cited)
  • A unique, verifiable solution
  • Include the full solution path
  • Rate the difficulty honestly
