FAQ

Questions, answered

Is ExtraLogic really free?

Yes. Every daily puzzle, game and the reasoning test are free, paid for by ads, sponsors and donations. ExtraLogic Pro (optional) removes ads and unlocks the full archive.

Is the Reasoning Test an official IQ test?

No. It's a practice test of logical reasoning skills, built for learning and fun. Official IQ tests are given in person by qualified psychologists.

When do new puzzles appear?

At midnight in your local time zone. Solve at least one puzzle a day to keep your streak alive.

Can I use ExtraLogic in my classroom or company?

Yes. See For Teams & Schools for classroom licenses, team events and custom assessments.

How do contests and prizes work?

Register free, then solve the contest set during the contest window. Rankings go by accuracy first, then time. Full rules are on the Contests page.
