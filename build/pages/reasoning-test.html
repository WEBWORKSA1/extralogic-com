About this test

How is the score calculated?

Each correct answer adds to a base score, with a small bonus for fast, accurate finishes. The result is mapped to a 70–150 scale where 100 is average performance. It is a practice indicator, not a standardised IQ.

Is my data private?

Answers are scored in your browser. If you choose to unlock the report, we store your name, email and score only to send you your results and, if you opted in, the newsletter. See our Privacy Policy.

How can I improve my score?

Practise daily: Number Sequences for numerical reasoning, Logic Challenges for deduction and Sudoku for pattern reasoning.
