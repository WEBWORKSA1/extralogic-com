Knights, Knaves & Logic Challenges

Truth-tellers, liars, syllogisms, fallacies and famous probability traps, each with a clear explanation.

How to play

Knights always tell the truth; knaves always lie. Assume a character is a knight and follow the consequences. If you reach a contradiction, they must be a knave. For syllogisms, draw the sets as circles.

FAQ

Who invented knights and knaves?

Logician Raymond Smullyan made them famous in 'What Is the Name of This Book?' (1978).
